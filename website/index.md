## Quick start

1. Download the jar and drop it in your Paper 26.1.2 server's `plugins/` folder (Java 25).
2. Restart the server.
3. Look at a block and run `/orbital classic`, or open `/orbital menu` to browse every cannon.

Made a mess? `/orbital undo`.
